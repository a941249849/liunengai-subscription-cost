How it works & privacy

Monthly billing: monthly equivalent = payment amount; annual total = amount × 12. Annual billing: monthly equivalent = amount ÷ 12; annual total = amount. Cost per active day = monthly equivalent ÷ active days per month.

No account, AI calls or live price feeds. Inputs are calculated in your current browser and are neither uploaded nor saved in local storage. This page contains no analytics or external scripts. Following links opens other pages with their own privacy and analytics practices.
